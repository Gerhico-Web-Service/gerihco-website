Frequently Asked Questions

What's the typical timeline for the hiring process?

Our standard hiring process includes an initial phone screen (15-20 minutes), followed by a technical interview (45-60 minutes), and then a final round with leadership (30-45 minutes). The entire process typically takes 1-2 weeks from initial contact to offer.

Do you offer remote work options?

Yes. We offer flexible hybrid arrangements with 2-3 days per week on-site at our office. We also consider remote arrangements for the right candidates, particularly for contractor and consulting roles.

What certifications do you prefer?

While certifications are valuable and we provide reimbursement, we focus primarily on hands-on experience and demonstrated expertise. Relevant certifications include CyberArk CCA, SailPoint, Okta, and Microsoft Entra ID credentials, as well as ISO/IEC and compliance-focused certs.

Do you sponsor visa holders or international candidates?

We currently hire primarily U.S. work-authorized candidates. We do not offer visa sponsorship at this time. Please confirm your work authorization status when applying.

What's your company culture like?

Gerihco is a focused, technical team committed to solving complex identity and access challenges for our clients. We value collaboration, continuous learning, and direct contribution. Our culture emphasizes practical problem-solving and professional accountability.

Can I apply for multiple positions?

Absolutely. If you're interested in multiple roles, you're welcome to apply for each. Simply note your preferences in your application. You can also email careers@gerihco.com with questions about which role best matches your background.
