Privileged Access Management (PAM)

Full-time

Compensation & Benefits

Competitive hourly pay • Medical, dental, and vision coverage • Life insurance & disability • 401(k) • HSA • Parking and transit benefits

Role Summary

Best for: Security specialists focused on protecting admin accounts and sensitive credentials. You'll manage the secrets vault, prevent unauthorized access to high-risk systems, and ensure password policies stay audit-ready.

What You'll Do Daily

  • Onboard and manage privileged accounts, service accounts, and application credentials into the PAM platform (CyberArk or similar).
  • Configure automatic password rotation, vault policies, and break-glass emergency access procedures.
  • Monitor privileged sessions in real-time and escalate suspicious activities.
  • Manage certificate and key lifecycles for critical systems.
  • Remediate audit findings and security risks tied to privileged access.
  • Document runbooks and operational procedures for audit and compliance teams.

You Should Apply If You Have

  • Hands-on experience with CyberArk, HashiCorp Vault, Delinea, or similar PAM platforms.
  • Strong understanding of privilege escalation, credential security, and attack paths.
  • Ability to work independently on complex onboarding tasks with minimal supervision.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in banking, financial services, or other highly regulated industries.
  • Professional certifications such as Security+, CISSP, CyberArk Defender, or cloud identity certifications.
  • Experience supporting audit, compliance, or risk management initiatives.

Education Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Computer Science, or a related field; equivalent professional experience may be considered.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to protect critical infrastructure and support security operations
