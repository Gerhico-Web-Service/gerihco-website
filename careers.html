Privileged Access Management (PAM) Engineer

Design, implement, and optimize enterprise privileged access controls for complex client environments. Work with CyberArk, Delinea, and enterprise infrastructure teams to secure sensitive systems and privileged identities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and implement PAM solutions for enterprise clients
  • Configure and manage CyberArk and Delinea platforms
  • Oversee account lifecycle, vaulting, and session monitoring
  • Develop automation scripts (Python, PowerShell, Bash)
  • Support troubleshooting and optimization initiatives

What We're Looking For

  • 7+ years in IT operations, security engineering, or infrastructure
  • 3+ years hands-on PAM platform experience
  • Strong Windows and Linux system administration skills
  • Proficiency with Python, PowerShell, or Bash
  • Excellent client-facing communication
Preferred Qualifications & Certifications
  • CyberArk Certified Associate (CCA) or higher
  • Delinea certification or equivalent
  • AWS, Azure, or GCP experience
  • NIST, CIS, or related security framework knowledge

Identity Governance & Administration (IGA) Engineer

Lead the design and delivery of governance solutions that help organizations manage access, reduce risk, and support compliance. Own implementation across SailPoint, Okta, Microsoft Entra, and related systems.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and implement IGA solutions using SailPoint, Okta, or Microsoft Entra
  • Manage user lifecycle provisioning and deprovisioning workflows
  • Lead access reviews, certifications, and governance reporting
  • Develop compliance dashboards and audit controls
  • Partner with security and audit teams

What We're Looking For

  • 7+ years in identity and access management roles
  • 3+ years hands-on IGA platform implementation
  • Strong understanding of user provisioning and access reviews
  • SQL and analytical reporting experience
  • Knowledge of SOX, HIPAA, GDPR, and ISO 27001 compliance
Preferred Qualifications & Certifications
  • SailPoint IdentityNow or IdentityIQ certification
  • Okta Admin or Developer certification
  • ServiceNow or enterprise system integration experience
  • Data governance and quality management background
