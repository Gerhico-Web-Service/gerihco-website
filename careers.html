cGMP Compliance Consultant / Auditor

Contract

Position Summary

The cGMP Compliance Consultant / Auditor will support a small non-manufacturing warehouse operation engaged in the storage, packaging, labeling, and distribution of dietary supplements and drug products. This role is responsible for assessing current compliance status, identifying practical remediation priorities, supporting gap closure, strengthening quality system documentation, and performing or supporting an independent third-party cGMP certification audit.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct a practical cGMP gap analysis against applicable FDA requirements, including 21 CFR Parts 210, 211, and 111, as relevant to warehousing, packaging, labeling, and distribution operations.
  • Review quality system documentation, including the Quality Manual, SOPs, training records, packaging and labeling procedures, receiving and shipping procedures, product traceability records, complaint files, and vendor qualification records.
  • Evaluate warehouse layout, material flow, segregation controls, inventory management practices, environmental controls, security controls, packaging areas, and label control systems.
  • Assess personnel roles, training records, defined responsibilities, and quality oversight functions.
  • Classify findings by risk level, including Critical, Major, Minor, and Opportunity for Improvement.
  • Develop corrective action recommendations, compliance roadmaps, CAPA plans, prioritization matrices, and implementation schedules.
  • Create or update controlled quality system documentation, SOPs, forms, records, and related procedures.
  • Support training on cGMP fundamentals, documentation practices, warehouse controls, packaging controls, and FDA inspection readiness.
  • Track CAPA implementation, verify closure of findings, and document effectiveness checks.
  • Conduct or support mock inspection readiness reviews and independent third-party certification audits.
  • Prepare executive-ready reports, audit findings logs, certification readiness assessments, and certification recommendations.

Required Qualifications

  • Demonstrated experience with FDA cGMP requirements applicable to drug products, dietary supplements, warehousing, packaging, labeling, and distribution.
  • Working knowledge of 21 CFR Parts 210, 211, and 111.
  • Experience conducting gap assessments, internal audits, supplier audits, mock inspections, or third-party certification audits.
  • Strong understanding of documentation control, training systems, supplier qualification, product traceability, complaint handling, returned goods controls, storage and distribution controls, change management, CAPA, and risk management.
  • Ability to develop practical, right-sized quality system documentation for small operations.
  • Strong written communication skills, including the ability to prepare executive summaries, compliance reports, audit reports, and corrective action plans.
  • Ability to work independently, prioritize risks, and provide hands-on implementation support.

Preferred Experience

  • Experience supporting small or lean life sciences, pharmaceutical, dietary supplement, or warehouse distribution operations.
  • Prior experience preparing organizations for FDA inspections or independent certification audits.
  • Experience developing Quality Manuals, SOP packages, CAPA trackers, audit checklists, training materials, and closure verification reports.
  • Experience assessing facilities where manufacturing, blending, compounding, or laboratory testing are not performed onsite.
  • Relevant quality, regulatory, auditing, or life sciences certifications are preferred.

Engagement Scope

The consultant or auditor will support a three-phase engagement consisting of a cGMP gap analysis, consulting services for gap closure, and an independent third-party cGMP certification audit. The work will focus on a single warehouse location with a small workforce and operations limited to storage, packaging, labeling, and distribution. The role does not include manufacturing validation, laboratory testing, product release decisions, legal advice, regulatory submissions, or direct representation before the FDA or other regulatory agencies.

Expected Deliverables

  • Comprehensive cGMP Gap Analysis Report
  • Executive Summary with risk-ranked findings
  • Corrective Action Recommendations and Compliance Roadmap
  • CAPA Tracker and Action Item Log
  • Updated Quality System Documentation and SOP Package
  • Training Records and training support materials
  • Closure Verification Report
  • Certification Readiness Assessment
  • Third-Party Audit Report and Audit Findings Log
  • Certification Recommendation and cGMP Compliance Certificate, if applicable

Success Measures

  • Critical findings are closed and documented.
  • Major findings are closed or supported by approved corrective action plans.
  • Quality system documentation is complete, controlled, and appropriate for the operation.
  • Personnel are trained and able to demonstrate competency in assigned responsibilities.
  • Warehouse, packaging, labeling, storage, distribution, traceability, complaint, return, supplier, and CAPA processes meet applicable cGMP expectations.
  • The operation is prepared for independent certification review and receives a favorable certification recommendation when requirements are met.
