Privileged Access Management (PAM) Engineer

Gerihco is seeking a PAM Engineer to design, implement, and optimize enterprise privileged access controls for complex client environments. This role is ideal for a security professional who enjoys solving high-impact identity and access challenges, working across critical infrastructure, and partnering with technical teams to reduce risk while improving operational resilience.

Responsibilities

  • Design and implement PAM solutions for enterprise clients and critical systems
  • Configure and manage leading platforms such as CyberArk and Delinea
  • Oversee privileged account lifecycle, vaulting, session monitoring, and access controls
  • Develop automation and integration scripts using Python, PowerShell, or Bash
  • Support troubleshooting, remediation, and optimization initiatives across client environments
  • Provide technical guidance, standards, and documentation for secure privileged access practices

Qualifications

  • 7+ years of experience in IT operations, security engineering, or infrastructure management
  • 3+ years of hands-on PAM platform experience, with CyberArk and/or Delinea strongly preferred
  • Strong knowledge of Windows and Linux system administration
  • Proficiency with scripting languages such as Python, PowerShell, or Bash
  • Experience with enterprise identity and access management principles
  • Excellent communication and client-facing skills
Preferred Qualifications
  • CyberArk Certified Associate (CCA) or higher
  • Delinea certification or equivalent
  • Experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP
  • Familiarity with NIST, CIS, or related security frameworks
Education Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or related field
  • Or equivalent professional experience demonstrating required competencies
Benefits & Work Arrangements
  • Competitive salary: $140K - $180K annually
  • Medical, dental, and vision insurance (company paid 80%)
  • 401(k) with 4% company match
  • Health Savings Account (HSA) with company contribution
  • Flexible hybrid work arrangement (2-3 days on-site)
  • Professional development budget ($2,500/year)
  • Certification reimbursement program
  • Generous paid time off (20 days + 10 holidays)

Identity Governance & Administration (IGA) Engineer

Gerihco is looking for an IGA Engineer to lead the design and delivery of governance solutions that help organizations manage access, reduce risk, and support compliance. This role is well suited to an experienced IAM professional who can translate complex access requirements into practical, scalable governance processes.

Responsibilities

  • Design and implement IGA solutions using SailPoint, Okta, Microsoft Entra, or similar platforms
  • Manage user lifecycle provisioning and deprovisioning workflows
  • Lead access reviews, certifications, and governance reporting
  • Develop compliance dashboards and analytics to support audit and control objectives
  • Create operational documentation, runbooks, and process guidance
  • Partner with security, audit, and IT teams to strengthen governance controls

Qualifications

  • 7+ years of experience in identity and access management roles
  • 3+ years of hands-on implementation experience with IGA platforms
  • Strong understanding of user provisioning, workflow design, access reviews, and governance controls
  • Experience with SQL and analytical reporting
  • Knowledge of compliance frameworks including SOX, HIPAA, GDPR, and ISO 27001
  • Strong problem-solving and stakeholder communication skills
Preferred Qualifications
  • SailPoint IdentityNow or IdentityIQ certification
  • Okta Admin or Developer certification
  • Experience integrating IAM solutions with ServiceNow or other enterprise systems
  • Background in data governance and quality management
Education Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or related field
  • Or equivalent professional experience demonstrating required competencies
Benefits & Work Arrangements
  • Competitive salary: $140K - $180K annually
  • Medical, dental, and vision insurance (company paid 80%)
  • 401(k) with 4% company match
  • Health Savings Account (HSA) with company contribution
  • Flexible hybrid work arrangement (2-3 days on-site)
  • Professional development budget ($2,500/year)
  • Certification reimbursement program
  • Generous paid time off (20 days + 10 holidays)
