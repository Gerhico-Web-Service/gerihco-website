Privileged Access Management (PAM) – Full-time Role

Full-time

Role Summary

Full-time PAM specialists support privileged account onboarding, credential vaulting, secrets management, and session monitoring. They execute operational tasks and project deliverables within the enterprise PAM program.

Responsibilities

  • Onboard privileged accounts, service accounts, and application credentials into the PAM platform.
  • Perform vaulting, password rotation, policy updates, and break glass configuration.
  • Monitor privileged sessions and escalate anomalies.
  • Support certificate, key, and secrets lifecycle activities.
  • Execute remediation tasks tied to audit findings or risk reduction initiatives.
  • Deliver PAM operational runbooks, documentation, and process improvements.

Required Skills

  • Hands on experience with CyberArk or equivalent PAM tooling.
  • Strong understanding of privileged account security and secrets management.
  • Ability to work independently on assigned onboarding and remediation tasks.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in banking, financial services, or other highly regulated industries.
  • Professional certifications such as Security+, CISSP, CyberArk, SailPoint, Okta, or cloud identity certifications.
  • Experience supporting audit, compliance, or risk management initiatives.

Education Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Computer Science, Business, or a related field; equivalent professional experience may be considered.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly compensation
  • Medical, dental and vision coverage offered
  • 401(k) participation options, subject to staffing partner plans
  • Professional development and enterprise IAM experience
  • Opportunity to support large-scale security and identity initiatives

Work Shift and Hybrid Expectations

  • Standard work schedule: Monday through Friday during core business hours.
  • Hybrid work arrangement with a combination of on-site and remote work based on business needs.
  • Participation in off-hours support, maintenance activities, or project deployments may be required on occasion.

Full-time

Role Summary
