Contract

Quality & Regulatory Compliance

cGMP Compliance Consultant / Auditor

Assess cGMP gaps in pharmaceutical and dietary supplement warehouse operations and support remediation and audit readiness.

Contract
Position Summary

Support a small, non-manufacturing warehouse operation with cGMP gap assessments, controlled documentation, corrective action planning, training, and audit-readiness reviews for storage, packaging, labeling, and distribution activities.

Key Responsibilities
  • Conduct a practical cGMP gap analysis against applicable FDA requirements, including 21 CFR Parts 210, 211, and 111.
  • Review quality system documentation, including Quality Manual, SOPs, training records, and packaging procedures.
  • Evaluate warehouse layout, material flow, segregation controls, inventory management, and environmental controls.
  • Classify findings by risk level and develop corrective action recommendations.
  • Create or update controlled quality system documentation and SOPs.
  • Support training on cGMP fundamentals and FDA inspection readiness.
  • Conduct mock inspection readiness reviews and support third-party certification audits.
Required Qualifications
  • Demonstrated experience with FDA cGMP requirements for drug products, dietary supplements, warehousing, packaging, and distribution.
  • Working knowledge of 21 CFR Parts 210, 211, and 111.
  • Experience conducting gap assessments, internal audits, and third-party certification audits.
  • Strong understanding of documentation control, training systems, supplier qualification, and CAPA.
