Benefits that support your health and well-being.

Gerihco offers a range of health, financial, and well-being benefits for eligible full-time employees. Explore the plans and resources available for the 2026–2027 plan year.

Employee benefits

The plan year runs from November 1, 2026, through May 31, 2027. Full-time employees become eligible for company-sponsored benefits on the first day of the month after their hire date.

Health and core coverage

  • Medical Choose from three Aetna medical plan designs, including an EPO and a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) with copays. Prescription drug coverage is included with medical coverage.
  • Health Savings Account (HSA) Employees enrolled in an HDHP may establish a pre-tax account for eligible health care expenses and may have investment options after meeting the account balance requirements.
  • Dental Coverage through an Aetna PPO plan.
  • Vision Coverage through the VSP Choice plan.
  • Basic life insurance $10,000 in basic life coverage through the plan.
  • Long-term disability Basic coverage replaces 50% of earnings, up to $1,000 per month, after a 180-day waiting period.

Spending and commuter accounts

  • Flexible Spending Accounts (FSAs) Medical reimbursement and dependent care accounts let employees set aside pre-tax dollars for eligible expenses.
  • Commuter benefits Set aside pre-tax payroll deductions for eligible commuting expenses and customize the account to your needs.
  • Domestic partner coverage Coverage may be available for an eligible employee's unmarried partner, subject to carrier rules. Tax treatment may apply when a partner is not a tax dependent.

Optional employee-paid coverage

  • Supplemental protection Optional hospital indemnity, critical illness, accident, voluntary term life, accidental death and dismemberment (AD&D), and short-term disability insurance.
  • Legal services Optional group legal plan with access to a nationwide attorney network and related resources.
  • Pet wellness and identity protection Optional Wagmo Pet Wellness and Norton LifeLock benefit plans.

Well-being, online services, and financial resources

  • Employee Assistance Program LifeCare EAP provides confidential support for personal, emotional, relationship, financial, legal, and work-life needs. Contact information and access instructions are available through the employee benefits portal.
  • Health Advocate Available to employees enrolled in an employer-sponsored medical plan to help manage care, resolve claims, and coordinate providers.
  • Employee discounts Discounts on products and services across categories such as travel, electronics, health, fitness, and cellular service.
  • Online HR profile The employee portal provides online access to pay statements and benefits enrollment, plus tools to update direct deposit and tax elections.
  • Retirement savings A 401(k) plan and investment options are available through the company's retirement plan.

Plan availability and terms are governed by the applicable official plan documents, which control if they differ from this overview.
