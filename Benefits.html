Benefits

Gerihco offers a comprehensive benefits package to support the health, financial security, and well-being of our full-time employees.

  • Medical Coverage through an Aetna high-deductible health plan (HDHP), paired with an Optum Health Savings Account (HSA).
  • Dental Coverage through an Aetna PPO plan.
  • Vision Coverage through the VSP Choice plan.
  • Commuter & transit Pre-tax commuter and transit benefit administered by Optum.
  • Life insurance Company-paid basic coverage ($10,000) through MetLife.
  • Long-term disability Company-paid coverage through MetLife, covering 50% of earnings up to $1,000 per month after a 180-day waiting period.

Benefits available to eligible full-time employees.
